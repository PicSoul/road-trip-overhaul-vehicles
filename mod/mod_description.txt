Real-world performance for the cars of the Road Trip: Ford DLC.

Weight, drag and engine output of every car are calibrated
so acceleration and top speed match the real car (with no
factory speed governor). Engines still show their real
power and torque figures.

Tyre grip is lowered for a realistic feel (old bias-ply tyres on
the Mustang grip least), so hills, grass and dirt need the right
drive mode and some care.

[orange]Ford Bronco 2024[normal]    0-60 mph ~6 s, ~130 mph
[orange]Ford F-150 2023[normal]     0-60 mph ~5.5-6 s, ~125 mph
[orange]Ford Mustang 1967[normal]   0-60 mph ~8.5-9 s, ~115-120 mph
[orange]Ford Crown Victoria 2006[normal]  0-60 mph ~7.5-8 s, ~130 mph

Requires the Road Trip: Ford DLC.
Changes apply to cars you already own.

Works best with the Road Trip Overhaul plugin (optional):
realistic throttle-based shifting, real 2H / 4H and
surface-dependent grip.
