[h1]Road Trip Overhaul - Vehicle Tuning[/h1]

Real-world performance for the cars of the [b]Road Trip: Ford[/b] DLC.

Weight, drag and engine output of every car are calibrated so acceleration and top speed match the real car (with no factory speed governor). Engines still show their real power and torque figures.

Tyre grip is lowered for a realistic feel (old bias-ply tyres on the Mustang grip least), so hills, grass and dirt need the right drive mode and some care.

Also fixes the '67 Mustang's missing tyre sound on concrete (a file name typo in the base game).

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]

[*]Road Trip: Ford DLC
[/list]

Changes apply to cars you already own. Only the Road Trip cars are changed; trucks are not affected. Optional in SCS Convoy.

[h2]Recommended (optional): Road Trip Overhaul plugin[/h2]
A separate SDK plugin with realistic throttle-based automatic shifting, real 2H / 4H on the Bronco and F-150 and surface-dependent tyre grip. The mod and the plugin each work on their own, or together.
